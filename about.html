Who I Am

About Me

Moved from Hungary to the Netherlands at 19, driven by the ambition to succeed and the belief that everything happens for a reason.

Background

I'm 21 years old, pursuing a bachelor's in Applied Data Science & Artificial Intelligence at Breda University of Applied Sciences in the Netherlands. Current GPA: 3.98 / 4.0.

I have a deep passion for solving complex data challenges with machine learning and AI — analyzing patterns, building predictive models, and uncovering insights that drive real decisions.

Programming excites me because it provides clarity and logic, where every challenge has a solution. Seeing my efforts translate into real results is incredibly rewarding. I believe AI is shaping the future, and I’m eager to contribute to solving emerging challenges in this dynamic field.

Beyond academics, I value a well-balanced lifestyle. Playing football, going for a run, or going to the gym keep me active and sharp, both mentally and physically.

"I am persistent and strongly believe that hard work and dedication make anything possible."

My Story

I was born and raised in Hungary, spending my early years in a small village where I attended elementary school. Later, I pursued my high school education at Varga Katalin High School in Szolnok, specializing in IT and Mathematics. It was during this time that I was introduced to programming, robotics, and linear algebra.

In 2023, I moved to the Netherlands to pursue my studies at Breda University of Applied Sciences. This was a significant step in my personal and professional growth, and living abroad has enriched my experiences, both academically and socially.

Outside of tech I'm a football enthusiast (the pro career didn't pan out, but the passion remains). I love travelling, exploring new cultures and cuisines, and I have a deep interest in architecture and tourism.

"I am excited about the future and look forward to the adventures that lie ahead."
